## The question

Before a charity commits to a big fundraising campaign, it needs to know whether the campaign can work. Why did finding that out take weeks of manual consultant interviews?

## What I found

The part I'm proudest of didn't start with making anything. It started with understanding how the work actually happened, finding the gaps and the patterns, and taking things out.

## What I tried

I built the Digital Feasibility Study, an AI-automated donor research product. An intelligent agent system does the work those weeks of interviews used to do.

## What it became

A net-new revenue stream for Global Philanthropic. Each engagement is worth £25,000, and it has delivered roughly £175,000 to £200,000 across seven or eight client engagements so far.

## The next question

If a charity can know this much before a campaign starts, what else has it been guessing?
