## The question

The company website had a UX performance score of 35 out of 100. Who was that failing?

## What I found

It was failing the people it was there to serve, and that wasn't something a new coat of paint could fix.

## What I tried

I rebuilt it from the structure up: a new information architecture, a hosting migration, and mobile-first performance work.

## What it became

The score went to 95, and the site now ranks strongly in search. It also became one of three websites I look after.

[Talking Philanthropy](/work/talking-philanthropy) has spent ten years convening the people who shape global giving. I maintain its website, so the event has a home that stays as current as the conversation.

[Philanthropic Intelligence](/work/philanthropic-intelligence) is a place to think clearly about how AI and a fast changing world apply to the way philanthropy is funded and led. I maintain its website, where those questions get worked through in the open.
