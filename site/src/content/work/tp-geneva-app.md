Talking Philanthropy held its first Summit in Geneva on 19 November 2026. The first guest app was a single page. Check-ins went into a record nobody could read on the day, and the door ran on iPads.

I rebuilt it as one app for the whole day. Guests check in with their name and organisation, which is matched against the confirmed in-person list, so nobody waits for an email. Each guest carries a QR pass on their phone. At the door, a volunteer scans it, and the name runs ahead to the desk in the order people were scanned. The lanyard is ready before the guest reaches it. Tap Given, and the name clears on every team phone.

The app follows the day. Before, Home counts down. On the morning, it shows the pass. During the sessions, it shows what's live and who's here who shares your interests. Afterwards, it asks for reflections. In between, guests message each other, send questions to the panels, and book time with the team, straight into the team's calendars. Speakers get a portal of their own, with their session and their contact on the team.

## What I learned

An event is a run of small waits. Most of the work was moving each one earlier: the check against the guest list before the door, the lanyard before the desk.
