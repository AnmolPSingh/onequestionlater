## The question

What happens to luxury fashion when scarcity is no longer about materials, but about digital exclusivity?

## What I found

I was part of a sustainability-focused fashion project when I realised nothing in fast fashion is truly sustainable. It felt like a cycle of greenwashing and half-measures.

## What I tried

JivaModa is an XR concept that moves past the usual sustainability story and follows that question instead.

## What it became

It was shortlisted for the XR Awards 2025.

Thanks to Gourav K. and Mohit V Bhardwaj for their guidance. Images in the project were sourced from DRESSX, which was also the inspiration.

## The next question
