## The question

Every non-profit leadership team is being asked to have a position on AI. How do you judge where it adds value, where it adds risk and how it should be governed?

## What I found

Few leaders have been given the tools to answer that. One set of people use AI like an advanced Google search. The other set wield it.

## What I tried

I run training sessions for the Catalyst cohort, women leading non-profits and impact organisations around the world. It starts with their organisation, not a curriculum, and their live projects and decisions become the learning. Leaders like Andrea Webster, Global Investor Lead at Climate Bonds Initiative.

## What it became

A move from literacy to judgement: knowing where AI belongs in their work, questioning what it produces, and governing its use. Most of what I do now is help people move from the first group to the second.

## The next question

What does a leader see differently once they stop using AI and start wielding it?
