# Story Arc and Interaction

*What happens on screen, section by section. The words live in `03 UX Copy`. The visuals live in `04 Design Direction` and `05 Assets Map`.*

## The shape

One page, one long scroll, eight chapters. Each chapter finishes the sentence "Be curious enough to...". It opens and closes the same way the ISDI talk did: a blank screen at the start, the same line at the end.

```
00  Opening            Be curious enough to...            (the sentence, unfinished)
01  Music              ...make a sound.
02  Photoshop, gigs    ...learn the tool.
03  Radio, film, news  ...ask the question nobody asks.
04  Freelance, UX      ...say yes before you know how.
05  The startup        ...be the only designer in the room.
06  ISDI               ...go back to school.
07  GP, AI             ...remove things.
08  Philanthropel      ...build what comes next.
--  The pattern        Eight careers, one pattern.
--  The door           Ask Anmol.
--  Close              Stay curious enough to find out.
```

Separate page: **The short version** (`/short-version`). Plain text, no animation, loads instantly.

## Rules that apply to every chapter

- **The motion graphic leads, the photo follows.** Each chapter opens on its motion graphic (the same family as the ISDI deck: waveform, posters, radio waves, film strip, shapes, app screens, path line, network). The photo drops in beside it as a small tilted print, like it did in the deck. The graphic is the story everyone can relate to. The photo is the proof it happened to Anmol.
- **The headline completes the sentence.** "Be curious enough to" stays small and fixed. The ending is the big line, and it's the first thing that animates in.
- **Scroll drives the animation, not time.** Nothing plays on a timer that the reader has to wait for. Scrolling back reverses it cleanly.
- **One pull line per chapter.** A single sentence pulled out large, in Anmol's voice. This is what people screenshot.
- **Chapter numbers 01 to 08 are real.** It's a sequence, so numbering earns its place.
- **Progress is always visible but quiet:** a thin rail of eight marks on the side (desktop) or along the bottom (mobile). Tap a mark to jump to that chapter.

## Section by section

### 00 Opening
- Full screen, dark, nothing else. After a beat, "Be curious enough to..." types out.
- The ending cycles through short words: "make a sound." "learn the tool." "ask why." "say yes." "remove things." "find out." Then it stops on the cursor.
- The intro line and the scroll cue fade in underneath.
- "The short version" and "Ask Anmol" sit top right from the first frame.

### 01 Music: make a sound
- Graphic: the live waveform from the deck, reacting to scroll position (and to the sound toggle if it's on).
- Photos: the audio engineering batch, then the festival set.

### 02 Learn the tool
- Graphic: the stacked gig posters, fanning out as you scroll.
- Photo: Delhi Raasta, 2014.
- Small detail: a fake "tool unlocked: Photoshop" tag that ticks on. This becomes a running motif. Each chapter unlocks the tool it needed (Photoshop, Premiere, UX, AI). Keep it subtle, like a game achievement, not a banner.

### 03 Ask the question nobody asks
- Graphic: radio waves rippling out, then the film strip runs across.
- Photos: the AIR desk (caption explains filming wasn't allowed), then the Doordarshan and BBC India short film.
- Tool unlocked: Premiere Pro.

### 04 Say yes before you know how
- Graphic: blank app screens that fill in as you scroll, as if being learned on the job.
- The "So naturally, I said yes." line lands on its own, with a pause before it (the reader scrolls into empty space, then it appears). Same timing as the joke in the talk.
- Tool unlocked: UX.

### 05 The only designer in the room
- Graphic: five labels (merchandising, development, tech, project management, marketing) orbiting one dot.
- Photos: the Delhi Fair name card, the "design for foreign markets" session, and the goofy office photo with "Not forgetting to have fun, here and there."

### 06 Go back to school
- Background switches from dark to paper. This is the change in mood, same as "Life at ISDI" in the deck.
- A loose collage that assembles as you scroll: Mumbai local, sketch wall, Andheri model, Maker's Studio, the workshop video, HoloLens, the cat.
- JivaModa gets its own moment: the JivaModa screen, large, with the XR Awards shortlist line.
- Ends on the final jury photos and "The best day I had there."

### 07 Remove things
- Back to dark.
- Graphic: a messy process diagram (lots of boxes and arrows) that simplifies as you scroll. Boxes fade out one by one until only a few remain, connected cleanly. This is the chapter's idea made visible: remove before you create.
- No GP screenshots, no numbers. Photo: Anmol at work (still needed).
- Tool unlocked: AI.

### 08 Build what comes next
- The Philanthropel logo render plays once and holds on the logo, as in the deck.
- Full circle: three frames appear one after another as you scroll: her walk, her TED talk, the Prime Video premiere.
- Link out to philanthropel.com.

### The pattern
- All eight chapter words line up in a row: Music. Radio. Film. Graphic design. UX. XR. AI.
- They dim, and one word stays lit: Curiosity.

### The door: Ask Anmol
- Calm, letter-like layout. Lots of space. Paper background.
- Shows how many spots are left this month ("3 of 5 questions open in November"). That number is what makes it feel exclusive.
- Below the form: "Answered", a small archive of questions he's answered and shared with permission.

### Close
- The opening returns: dark screen, "Stay curious enough to find out." types out.
- Footer: LinkedIn, email (as plain text), Philanthropel.

## Interactions outside the story

- **Calm mode:** a toggle in the corner. Turns off all scroll animation and the type effect. Also on automatically when the device asks for reduced motion.
- **Sound (optional):** off by default. When on, a quiet loop of one of Anmol's own tracks plays and the waveform in chapter 01 follows it. Must be his own music, never a licensed track.
- **Keyboard:** arrow keys and space move chapter to chapter. Every link and form field has a visible focus state.
