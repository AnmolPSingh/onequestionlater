# Site Brief: curiousenough.com

*The why and the rules. Read this before anything else in this folder.*

## What this site is

A story, not a portfolio and not a CV. One long scroll that tells how one question kept turning into the next thing Anmol did: music, radio, film, design, UX, a startup, ISDI, AI, Philanthropel. It ends at a door where people can ask him for advice.

The organising idea is the name. curiousenough.com describes me, and the site turns it into an unfinished sentence: "Be curious enough to...". Every chapter finishes it differently. The word doing the work is "enough": the point where curiosity turns into action.

Domains: curiousenough.com is the main address. becuriousenough.com is also owned and redirects to it.

## Who it's for

1. **People who've met Anmol** (at a talk, a workshop, a client meeting) and want to know who he is. They arrive curious and have a few minutes.
2. **People who want his advice** on design, AI, or careers that don't run in a straight line. They're heading for the Ask Anmol door.
3. **People evaluating him** (assessors, collaborators, partners). They have about ten seconds. They get "The short version" link, always visible.

## What it must do

- Tell the story in a way that feels like Anmol talking, not a bio written about him.
- Let the motion graphics carry the story and use his photos as proof beside them. The site is about the pattern, not about him posing. (Same rule as the ISDI deck.)
- Give the impatient reader a way out: "The short version" page, plain and fast.
- Turn "I keep giving advice here and there" into a proper, slightly exclusive place to ask.

## Non-negotiables

- **All story text is real HTML text**, never baked into images or video. Search engines, screen readers and anyone evaluating him need to read it.
- **No Global Philanthropic internal numbers or client details.** No revenue, workload percentages, UX scores, proposal counts, client names or screenshots of internal systems. Describe the thinking, not the metrics.
- **No "open to work", no job-hunting signals.** It's a story and an invitation to talk, not a pitch.
- **Respects reduced motion.** Everything reads the same with animation off.
- **Works on a phone.** Most people will open it from a LinkedIn link on mobile.
- **Voice:** first person, warm and direct, short connected sentences, no em dashes, no corporate words. Source of truth: `Cowork Os/00_Resources/voice-principles.md`.
- **Handle with care** (from the origin story file): no mention of cracking Photoshop; describe early posters as learning by rebuilding, not copying.
