## Use with care

- `srishti-ted.jpg`: if it's a frame from the TED video, it's TED's material. Swap it for a photo Srishti owns, or use the text line without a picture.
- Every photo with other people in it (batchmates, startup colleagues, jury members): fine for a personal story site, but take down any photo someone asks you to remove.

## Formats at build time

- Photos: AVIF and WebP, 1x and 2x, max width 1600px. Strip EXIF location data.
- Videos: MP4 (H.264) and WebM, muted, no audio track, with a poster frame.
- Keep a `credits` note for any image you didn't take yourself.
